The Music Technology Reading Group (MTRG) is a freely organized network of musicologists, philosophers, computer scientists, engineers, and computer music researchers interested in music and technology. The researchers are located in Sweden, France, Norway, Slovenia, and South Korea.

Since May 2024, we have convened online once a month to read and discuss mainly humanities-oriented scholarship on the impacts of technologies on aspects of music culture, practices, ideologies, aesthetics, creativity, and more. We also read texts that consider technologies more broadly and discuss how these may be applied in musical contexts. From early recording technology to artificial intelligence, the interests of MTRG span across centuries of music technological thought.
